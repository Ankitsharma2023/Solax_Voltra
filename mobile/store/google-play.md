## Full description (max 4000)
Voltra Energy is the companion app for solar and battery systems installed by Voltra. See exactly what your system is doing, right now and over time.

LIVE ENERGY FLOW
• Watch power move between your solar panels, battery, the grid and your home
• Battery charge level, charging or discharging, and remaining energy
• Updated every few minutes from your inverter

STATISTICS & REPORTS
• Daily, monthly, yearly and lifetime generation
• Energy graphs for solar, consumption, battery and grid import/export
• Earnings and savings using your own electricity tariff
• Environmental benefits: CO₂ saved and trees planted
• Export reports as CSV

DEVICES
• Inverter, battery and Wi-Fi dongle details and live readings
• Device curves and history
• Alarms and alerts history

SMART FEATURES
• Live Data for every reading of your system
• Work mode, time-of-use planning and automation (when enabled by Voltra)
• Share your system with family

FOR INSTALLERS & PARTNERS
• Portfolio overview of all your plants: status, generation and capacity
• Alarms across all sites, plant search and filters
• Add new installations by scanning the inverter and dongle labels

GETTING STARTED
Sign up with your e-mail and the inverter serial number and Wi-Fi dongle registration number printed on your devices — or scan their labels with the camera.

Voltra Energy works with SolaX inverters and batteries installed and registered by Voltra. Need help? Contact info@voltra.in or +91 99929 29203.
