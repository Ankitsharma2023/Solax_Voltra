2. Who can see your data

  • You — your own plant(s) and account.
  • Your installer and their distributor — the plants linked to them, so they can service your system.
  • Voltra staff (admins) — all plants and accounts, for support and operations.
  • Service providers that run the App for us, only as needed: SolaX Power (energy data), MongoDB Atlas (account database), Render (hosting), the push services of Google/Apple/Mozilla (notifications), MET Norway / Open-Meteo (weather for your plant's location)[, e-mail provider once chosen].
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

3. Where it is stored

Account and settings data are stored in MongoDB Atlas [region — confirm in the Atlas project]; the App runs on Render (Singapore). Energy data stays in SolaX Cloud and is read when you open the App, with a short-lived cache. Data may therefore be processed outside India.

4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted within [30] days after you ask us to close it. Inverter command records are kept for [2 years] for safety and warranty reasons. Technical logs are kept for [30 days].

5. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details in the App (Me), or ask us for a copy.
  • Turn notifications off at any time (Me → Notifications).
  • Ask us to delete your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent — this closes your account, as the App cannot work without it.
  • Complain to us, and if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Contact our grievance officer: [name], [e-mail], [phone].

6. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, connections use HTTPS, the SolaX key never leaves our server, each account only sees the plants it is allowed to, repeated wrong logins are blocked, and every inverter command is checked, confirmed and logged.

7. Children

The App is meant for system owners and businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the App.
