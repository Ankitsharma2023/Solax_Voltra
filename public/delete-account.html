Delete your Voltra account

Voltra Energy app · Voltra Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Voltra Energy app (phone or website) and log in.
  2. Go to Me → General Settings → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are logged out.

By e-mail

If you can't log in, write to info@voltra.in from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Voltra account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, e-mail, phone, password, company details.
  • Your app settings and notification subscriptions, and the link between your account and your solar system.
  • Your messages in the app.

What is kept

  • Records of inverter commands sent to your system are kept for 2 years for safety and warranty reasons, then deleted.
  • Technical logs are kept for 30 days.
  • Your system's energy data belongs to the system and stays in SolaX Cloud, where your installer manages it.
